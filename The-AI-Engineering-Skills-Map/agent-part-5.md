# The AI Engineering Skills Map — Part 5: Shaping the Build

**Source:** Andrew Ng and DeepLearning.AI, “The AI Engineering Skills Map Part 5 — Shaping the Build”

## Purpose

AI engineering expands a developer’s role beyond implementing a specification. Developers can help decide what to build, while product managers and designers can also take part in building. This broader participation can speed development and gives engineers more responsibility for project direction and results.

## Four skills for shaping the build

1. **Drive the build loop.** Keep moving from implementation to feedback to the next decision. Choose whether to prototype a technical idea, test an MVP with users, add features, or invest in an enterprise-ready system. Ship in small batches and use user feedback or technical experiments to decide what to do next. Consider product vision, project stage, feasibility, risks, effort, and budget. For mature products, define metrics and organize work to improve them.

2. **Make product decisions.** Fill gaps the specification does not cover, and create a specification when needed. Use product sense to choose directions that address real user needs, design sense to make products pleasant to use, and business sense to consider go-to-market, market size, unit economics, and profitability. Build user empathy through methods suited to the question, from informal interviews and surveys to A/B tests and analysis of product behavior.

3. **Communicate and lead.** Work effectively with stakeholders across functions such as marketing, finance, and legal. Align people, coordinate decisions, and communicate technical feasibility and the possibilities created by AI. Clear communication also supports better conversations with users and helps organizations understand how AI may affect their work.

4. **Take high-agency ownership.** Identify valuable problems and opportunities, propose solutions, and act without waiting for detailed direction, while respecting organizational priorities and constraints. Own initiatives end to end, take responsibility for issues, work through ambiguity and setbacks, and measure success by the value created rather than by task completion alone. Keep improving by following new technology, learning tools, and refining workflows.

## Main takeaway

AI engineering gives developers more scope to influence what gets built and how it creates value. Taking advantage of that opportunity calls for product judgment, user empathy, communication, initiative, and continued learning alongside technical skill.
